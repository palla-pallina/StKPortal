    FEAST OF THE DAY | March 25, 2025

    The Solemnity of the Annunciation, celebrated on March 25, honors the angel Gabriel’s message to Mary that she would bear Jesus. Her faithful "yes" began God’s plan of salvation. This feast, observed since the 7th century, reminds us of the power of trust in God and inspired prayers like the Ave Maria and Angelus.
